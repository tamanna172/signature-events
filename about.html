Our story

The studio behind the seating chart

Ten years, three planners, and a filing cabinet of run sheets that have never once been needed twice.

Started with one borrowed marquee

Signature Events Co. began in 2016 when our founder, Amara, planned her sister’s garden wedding with a borrowed marquee, a spreadsheet and a stubborn belief that great events are built on logistics, not luck. Word travelled. The spreadsheet became a studio.

Today we are a deliberately small team in Surry Hills managing around forty events a year. Small is the point: every client works directly with a lead planner who carries their event from the first coffee to the last candle.

What we value

How we work

Preparation over performance. Anyone can look busy on the day. We would rather be quietly redundant, because the timing, the weather plan and the supplier briefings were settled weeks earlier.

Your taste, amplified. We do not arrive with a house style and make your event wear it. We listen first, then build a look and a room that feel unmistakably like you.

Honest budgets. Every quote is itemised and every change is priced before it happens. Money conversations should be the least dramatic part of any celebration.

Kindness under pressure. Events are made by people: caterers, florists, drivers, in-laws. We treat all of them well, and it shows in how smoothly a room runs.
