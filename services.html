Services

Everything a great event needs

Book one service or hand us the whole occasion. Either way, the same planner answers your calls.

Our six services

Wedding planning

Full planning or final-month takeover: celebrant, styling, timeline and a calm voice beside you at the altar. From elopements to 250 guests.

Corporate events

Conferences, galas, launches and end-of-year parties with AV, staging and speaker wrangling that keep your brand looking sharp.

Private parties

Milestone birthdays, anniversaries and engagements. You pick the guest list and the playlist; we handle everything with a clipboard.

Catering & décor

Menus, florals, linen and lighting sourced from our supplier family and styled to a single, coherent look for your room.

Venue sourcing

Shortlists matched to your guest count, budget and weather plan, with site walk-throughs booked and contract terms checked.

Full coordination

The complete package: every service above under one plan, one budget and one on-the-day team, from first idea to final farewell.
